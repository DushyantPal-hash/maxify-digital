The Challenge

Baggage mishandling, delayed updates, and non-compliance with aviation safety regulations can result in significant operational inefficiencies and dissatisfied passengers.

Our Solution

MaxBRS tackles these challenges with cutting-edge technology

  • Track Every Bag: Real-time tracking of baggage location at the airport, gate, and aircraft.
  • Passenger Match: Verify every piece of baggage is positively matched to a verified passenger before loading.
  • Error-Free Operations: Automated notifications and error messages ensure no misplaced baggage or loading errors.
