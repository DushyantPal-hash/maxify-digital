MaxPRS: The Smarter Solution for Airports and Airlines

Key Features Designed for Ground Staff and Airport Operations:

Integrated Android App

Intuitive app on handheld devices for scanning passenger boarding passes and reconciling airline staff check-ins and check-outs. Convenient offline mode ensures uninterrupted operations in connectivity-restricted zones.

Flight and Persona Manifest Synchronization

Real-time updates and persona data directly from DCS for accurate passenger reconciliation.

Advanced Barcode Technology

Simplifies scanning and reconciliation, minimizing manual input errors.

Background Web Services

Ensures seamless synchronization between the app server and handheld device databases, keeping data accurate and up-to-date.

Multi-Flight Boarding Support

Effortlessly manage boarding processes for multiple flights simultaneously.

Comprehensive Reporting

Generate real-time reports on checked-in and boarded passengers, improving transparency and decision-making.
