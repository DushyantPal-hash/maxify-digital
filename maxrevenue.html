Key Revenue & Analytics KPIs for Airlines

To measure the success of CRM solutions in optimizing airline revenue, track the following KPIs:

Revenue per Available Seat Kilometer (RASK)

Tracks revenue generated per seat for a given distance.

Load Factor Optimization

Measures the percentage of available seating capacity filled with paying passengers.

Ancillary Revenue Per Passenger (ARPP)

Calculates revenue from non-ticket sources like baggage and premium services.

Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

Evaluates the total revenue expected from a customer throughout their relationship with the airline.

Revenue Leakage & Fraud Prevention Rate

Monitors revenue loss due to inefficiencies, fraud, and errors.

Cost per Passenger (CPP)

Assesses operational efficiency per traveler.

Sales Conversion Rate

Measures the effectiveness of marketing campaigns.

Revenue Forecast Accuracy

Evaluates the accuracy of revenue projections.

Market Share Growth

Tracks airline revenue growth in competitive markets.

Profitability per Route

Identifies the most profitable routes for strategic adjustments.
