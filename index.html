MaxBag: Salesforce Native Baggage Solutions for Airlines, Airports and Aviation

MaxBag, built natively on Salesforce, is an AI-powered baggage solution that optimizes baggage handling and boosts customer satisfaction. By leveraging advanced algorithms for baggage matching and standardized case reporting, MaxBag ensures a seamless and efficient baggage management experience while improving operational efficiency and reducing lost baggage incidents.

MaxBRS: Baggage Reconciliation System

The baggage reconciliation system tracks baggage location within the airport, at the gate, and on the plane, and matches all loaded bags with passengers. It verifies whether a piece of baggage can be positively matched to a verified boarded passenger before it is loaded onto a departing aircraft.

MaxPRS: Persona & Passenger Reconciliation System for Ground Staff & Airport Operations

MaxPRS is a powerful solution designed to enhance passenger reconciliation and improve airline, airport and aviation operations.
